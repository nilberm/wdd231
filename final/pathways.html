Immigration Pathways

A short overview of the main ways skilled workers move to Canada. Rules and processing times change, so always confirm details on the official government website.

Express Entry

Express Entry is the federal system that manages applications for permanent residence from skilled workers. It covers the Federal Skilled Worker Program, the Federal Skilled Trades Program, and the Canadian Experience Class.

  1. Check that you meet the requirements of at least one program.
  2. Create an online profile and receive a Comprehensive Ranking System score.
  3. Wait for an invitation to apply during a draw.
  4. Submit your complete application for permanent residence.

Provincial Nominee Programs

Most provinces and territories run their own programs to select workers who match local labour needs. A nomination can add a large number of points to an Express Entry profile. Each city page lists the main program for its province.

Work permits

A work permit lets you work in Canada for a limited time. Many permits are tied to a specific employer, while open work permits allow you to work for almost any employer. Canadian work experience can later help your permanent residence application.

Official information: Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada.
